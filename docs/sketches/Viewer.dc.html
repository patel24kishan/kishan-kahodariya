Click the card image
Viewer opens on screenshot 1. Arrows, thumbnails or arrow keys move through the rest.
Click "Gameplay"
Same viewer opens with the video playing. YouTube links are embedded; uploaded files play directly.
Click "View code"
Opens the repo in a new tab. GitHub and GitLab do not allow their pages to be shown inside another site.
Close with the X, the Esc key, or a click outside. Keyboard focus stays inside the viewer while it is open and returns to the card on close; videos carry captions or a text description. The backdrop is black at 70% opacity, so the page stays visible behind it and keeps its scroll position. In admin, each project gets a list of screenshots (upload or paste URL, drag to reorder), one gameplay video URL, and an editable hover text for its card (4 words at most; blank uses the default).
