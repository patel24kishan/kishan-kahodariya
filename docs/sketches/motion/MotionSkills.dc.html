How it moves
1The rows stay put. Nothing scrolls sideways and nothing keeps moving.
2When the section scrolls into view, each row's line draws across, the group name fades up, and its skills arrive one after another. This plays once and then stops.
3Hovering a row lights up that group in the accent colour.
4On a phone the group name sits above its skills. With reduced motion on, everything is simply there.
On this board the entrance replays every 9 seconds so you can watch it. Hover a row above to try the highlight.
